# Free card catalogs and price evidence for the macOS auction companion

Checked: **2026-10-31 UTC**. Every linked external source below was checked on this date. Research for issue `01-free-card-and-price-data`; evidence and recommendations, not a product decision or permission to implement.

Environment verified: `devbox`, account `t3`, checkout `/home/t3/.t3/worktrees/not-tcg/t3-1069c73f`, prepared branch `t3/free-card-price-data`, starting revision `bfd3826cd6ece7f4db87c9382503baaa912638fe`. No remote is configured. This report is the only changed file.

## Verdict

**A free catalog is feasible; a dependable, permission-established free numeric pricing path covering both raw and graded English singles is not established by this investigation.** TCGdex is the strongest catalog candidate, subject to printing verification and artwork rights. Cardmarket offers an actual public marketplace price export, but it cannot establish English, condition-specific or slab-specific values. Details and direct evidence follow.

There is a meaningful conditional alternative: PokemonPriceTracker explicitly offers free raw and graded **aggregates** for non-revenue tools. It also expressly declines to represent that it has rights in its upstream data, and individual sold-listing detail requires Business. Therefore, “no free graded data exists” would be inaccurate, but “credible, licensed free graded comps are solved” would also be inaccurate. JustTCG has a promising graded beta, but its published distribution guidance requires payment when shipping to other users. Neither is a confirmed launch dependency.

**Launch conflict:** both raw and slabs remain required. Insufficient graded evidence must lead to a human decision about the evidence standard, a further no-cost permission/data path, or an explicit change to the launch promise. It does not authorize silently shipping raw-only pricing.

## Method and limits

- Read the assigned issue and map in full. Used provider documentation, terms, public source code, official announcements and anonymous public data endpoints. Search results from third-party sites were discovery aids only.
- No accounts, keys, paid calls, capture sessions, GUI work, messages or host changes. Free-key onboarding below is **provider-advertised**, not tested registration. No authenticated price endpoint was exercised.
- Five small anonymous checks covered two card records, two repository commit records and TCGCSV's build timestamp; one public Cardmarket bulk export was also read. These establish availability at one instant, not coverage, accuracy, service reliability or latency percentiles.
- “Sourced fact” below means the publisher documents it; accuracy and source authorization are separate questions. “Observed” means an anonymous response was inspected. Proposals and unmeasured hypotheses are labeled.

## Candidate-by-candidate findings

### TCGdex — provisional catalog; conditional raw reference; no documented slab prices

**Access and identity.** Its [FAQ](https://tcgdex.dev/faq) says the API is free without a key, publishes no hard request limits and encourages local caching. The same FAQ acknowledges incorrect marketplace mappings, different rarities mapping to the same price, missing prices and an unfinished `variants_detailed` improvement. Treat these as current limitations, not promises already delivered.

The [card schema](https://tcgdex.dev/reference/card) provides set/card IDs, number, names, rarity, optional images and availability flags for normal/reverse/holo/first edition. Provider pricing has its own `updated` field; card `updated` excludes pricing. TCGplayer blocks contain variant-level low/mid/high/market values, not condition-specific transactions or grader/grade fields. An availability flag does not identify the printing currently visible in a video.

**Prices and provenance.** The [market integration](https://tcgdex.dev/markets-prices) advertises hourly-to-daily USD TCGplayer values and daily EUR Cardmarket values. These are aggregates/guide values, not a list of sold comps. The pinned [TCGplayer provider selector](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tcgdex/cards-database/ddaf9e1baf1275d893a1e8a60432619a6410206e/server/src/libs/providers/tcgplayer/index.ts) can choose a proxy, direct access or TCGCSV; the [TCGCSV adapter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tcgdex/cards-database/ddaf9e1baf1275d893a1e8a60432619a6410206e/server/src/libs/providers/tcgplayer/tcgcsv.ts) propagates its build time. This proves possible implementations, not which production upstream is active or licensed. The [Cardmarket provider](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tcgdex/cards-database/ddaf9e1baf1275d893a1e8a60432619a6410206e/server/src/libs/providers/cardmarket.ts) reads Cardmarket's public guide.

**Rights and maintenance.** The [repository MIT license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tcgdex/cards-database/master/LICENSE) permits reuse of covered material with its copyright/license notice. It does not establish sublicensing of marketplace feeds or Pokémon artwork. [Image documentation](https://tcgdex.dev/assets) supplies high/low resolution asset URLs, not a separate artwork grant. Observed repository activity: [commit ddaf9e1](https://github.com/tcgdex/cards-database/commit/ddaf9e1baf1275d893a1e8a60432619a6410206e), dated October 30, 2026. Source activity is not a pricing SLA.

**Unresolved:** full printing coverage, image display/cache/bundling rights, upstream TCGplayer redistribution permission, shipping/tax basis, condition basis, numerical rate ceilings and service continuity. Source-specific links and attribution should be preserved; no marketplace rights should be inferred from MIT.

### Pokémon TCG API (`pokemontcg.io`) — functioning legacy data, unsuitable new dependency

The [documentation banner](https://docs.pokemontcg.io/) now says the project is deprecated, new registrations are closed and existing keys work through March 1, 2027. The owner's [data-repository README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokemonTCG/pokemon-tcg-data/master/README.md) explicitly schedules the API to go offline that day and recommends no new integrations. This supersedes older tutorials describing readily available free keys.

The still-published [limits](https://docs.pokemontcg.io/getting-started/rate-limits/) are 20,000 requests/day with a key and 1,000/day, maximum 30/minute without one. The [card object](https://docs.pokemontcg.io/api-reference/cards/card-object/) has IDs, set/number, images, TCGplayer URLs and USD variant aggregates, plus Cardmarket URLs and EUR aggregates, each with `updatedAt`. These inherited fields are not direct TCGplayer access, licensed sold records or slab pricing. No grader/grade or per-sale timestamps are documented there.

Observed: the anonymous endpoint still answered successfully; see the snapshot table below. [Latest commit inspected](https://github.com/PokemonTCG/pokemon-tcg-data/commit/39a26a144c8b6ef6c2fb17b2c29d0bb7121e3a11) was dated September 17, 2026. Neither observation cancels the retirement notice. Pricing/image redistribution rights, shipping basis and a durable free successor are unestablished.

### Direct TCGplayer — unavailable to newly registered developers

[Getting Started](https://docs.tcgplayer.com/docs/getting-started) explicitly closes new API access and requires existing developer credentials. A [September 15, 2026 announcement](https://seller.tcgplayer.com/blog/building-a-more-scalable-and-secure-api-program-at-tcgplayer) describes reviews and changes even for existing users. Do not promise new-user keys, borrow legacy credentials or assume a wrapper transfers access.

[Condition standards](https://help.tcgplayer.com/hc/en-us/articles/221430307-Card-Conditioning-Overview) cover NM, LP, MP, HP and Damaged; marketplace condition is not a slab grade. TCGplayer defines [Market Price as recent-sale aggregation for a product/condition](https://help.tcgplayer.com/hc/en-us/articles/201914668-Best-Practices-for-Pricing-Your-Items), distinct from current listings and its last-sold price-plus-shipping metric. An intermediary's product-level `market` field does not thereby become condition-specific sold evidence.

The [API terms](https://help.tcgplayer.com/hc/en-us/articles/360061115874-TCGplayer-API-Terms-Conditions), updated June 8, 2022, require approved purposes and prohibit key transfer, price-data combination, unauthorized automated collection and obtaining content from third parties collected through the API or automation. They require source attribution, a product/search link and a prominent non-endorsement notice. This is a substantive permission question for inherited data, not an allegation about a particular provider. Written upstream authorization is unverified. No usable new-user quota, caching grant or graded-sales route was established.

### eBay — possible active-listing reference; free sold-comp integration not established

The official [developer registration page](https://developer.ebay.com/signin) advertises free membership and 5,000 calls/day. The [EPN questionnaire](https://partnernetwork.ebay.com/page/developer-questionnaire) says Browse needs no additional approval, while Marketplace Insights cannot be granted upon request. Broader [Buy requirements](https://edp.ebay.com/api-docs/buy/buy-requirements.html) describe application reviews for restricted access. Thus a developer account is not proof of access to sold history. Marketplace Insights documentation redirected to sign-in during this check; no new-user access was tested. eBay's [deprecation table](https://developer.ebay.com/develop/api-deprecation-status) dates Finding API retirement to February 4, 2025; old `findCompletedItems` recipes are not a solution.

The current [Browse search reference](https://www.developer.ebay.com/develop/api/buy) describes listing prices, current bids, item URLs, currency and shipping information; default results are fixed-price listings. These are active offers/bids, not realized sales. Seller-entered titles/aspects need printing, language, condition and slab-label validation; eBay is not a canonical Pokémon catalog.

The [API agreement](https://developer.ebay.com/join/api-license-agreement) prohibits using eBay content to suggest/model prices for items listed on eBay, requires isolated public display and deletion of no-longer-public content, and caps displayed listing staleness at six hours and other content at 24 hours. Application to this companion's exact use needs resolution before integration; per-user keys do not waive it. The [official native-client guidance](https://github.com/eBay/ebay-oauth-android-client) also requires protecting client secrets through a backend in its documented user-authorization flow; it is not evidence of a supported Mac BYO-key arrangement.

**Human research fallback:** [Product Research](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling-tools/research?id=4853) documents three years of sales, accepted Best Offer prices and shipping metrics for sellers with Seller Hub access. That human-facing entitlement is not an API/export license or a guarantee that every buyer has access. A user-opened research link can help with slabs without importing the result into the app.

### PriceCharting — useful human reference; excluded as a free API dependency

Its [API documentation](https://www.pricecharting.com/api-documentation) requires a paid subscription. API/CSV output is current valuation, not historical sales or historical prices. It documents USD cents, one call/second, daily CSV generation and internal-use licensing; sharing through an application requires commercial licensing and express written permission. Caching is encouraged under that license, with deletion after subscription termination. A personal subscription token alone does not establish distributed-client rights. Fields include raw and grade bands, PSA 10 and additional named top-grade tiers; generic grade fields must not be relabeled as a specific grader.

The [methodology](https://www.pricecharting.com/page/methodology) uses sold data and proprietary calculations; some grade bands pool PSA/BGS, and listed prices exclude shipping and other transaction costs. Public pages are candidates for user-opened research. No automated extraction or free price-data license was established. Exact timestamps, condition-specific raw coverage and attribution beyond the API's requested linkback would need source-specific confirmation if scope ever changes.

### Cardmarket public exports — the strongest direct free raw aggregate path, with major matching limits

The owner's [June 2024 launch announcement](https://news.cardmarket.com/en/Magic/were-making-the-price-guide-and-product-catalogue-available-for-download) makes files available for all games without API access, with daily guide updates and catalog updates at new releases. Its earlier [platform announcement](https://insight.cardmarket.com/en/Articles/the-state-of-cardmarket-2024) explicitly describes using these datasets in websites/apps. This is stronger provenance than an unexplained wrapper. Direct authenticated [API applications remain closed](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/cardmarket-api).

Observed: the [Pokémon guide JSON](https://downloads.s3.cardmarket.com/productCatalog/priceGuide/price_guide_6.json) returned HTTP 200 without credentials, version 1, `createdAt: 2026-10-31T02:42:33+0100`, and 80,173 records. Inspected record keys include product/category IDs, low/average/trend, 1/7/30-day averages and holo equivalents. They provide **no language, condition, grader, grade, shipping, individual sale, sale URL or sale timestamp fields**. It is therefore impossible to establish an English NM value or PSA 10 comparable from that record alone. An English catalog name does not make the underlying price English-only. TCGdex's pinned provider above labels these values EUR.

The [Pokémon download page](https://www.cardmarket.com/en/Pokemon/Data/Price-Guide) returned 403 in the research fetcher; the old [API guide](https://api.cardmarket.com/ws/documentation/API_2.0:PriceGuide) returned 410. Product-catalog schema and complete printing joins were not validated. Current numerical request limits, mandatory attribution, shipping/tax methodology and bulk redistribution/bundling terms were not established. The permissive announcement supports investigation, not an invented comprehensive license. No graded export was found.

### TCGCSV — free raw mirror, unsuitable direct desktop dependency without further permission/design work

The [homepage](https://tcgcsv.com/) describes TCGplayer catalog/product/variant aggregates updated daily around 20:00 UTC; it deliberately omits SKUs, hence condition-specific prices. The [FAQ](https://tcgcsv.com/faq) says individual sales/listings are unavailable, provides daily price archives from February 8, 2024 and requires no TCGCSV attribution. Archives are snapshots, not sales histories. These statements do not grant upstream TCGplayer or image rights.

The [usage rules](https://tcgcsv.com/docs) require daily ingestion/cache, a distinct User-Agent and at least 100 ms between requests; over 10,000 requests/day may be banned. Crucially, they instruct developers not to request directly from user-facing applications. Treat a background updater inside a distributed desktop app as an unresolved interpretation, not an automatic exception. A central mirror would introduce additional distribution responsibilities. Observed [build marker](https://tcgcsv.com/last-updated.txt): `2026-10-31T20:05:59+0000`. This does not prove the underlying prices refreshed then. USD raw aggregates, no graders; source item mapping, shipping and upstream rights remain gates.

### PokemonPriceTracker — conditional free raw/graded aggregates; no free auditable sold records

The [pricing page](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/pricing) advertises 100 credits/day, 60 requests/minute, three days of history and raw/PSA data. Its feature bullets and comparison table disagree about commercial inclusion; rely on the binding terms rather than the favorable bullet. The [API reference](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/api-reference) advertises immediate free keys without a card, English by default, daily updates, one credit/card plus one for graded data, and per-printing graded aggregates. At those documented costs, at most 50 single-card basic-plus-graded lookups/day fit 100 credits before other usage; this is arithmetic, not measured capacity. Expanded schemas were not fully exposed by the research fetcher; `/docs` only rendered a loading placeholder.

The [FAQ](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/faq) claims PSA/CGC/BGS/SGC sales aggregates including half grades, and acknowledges interpolation where sales are sparse. A grade label or smooth chart therefore cannot be treated as an observed sale. Full grade/qualifier, raw-condition, timestamp and printing accuracy on a new Free account remain untested.

The binding [terms, §6](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/terms), effective October 13, 2026 for new accounts, explicitly allow free non-revenue applications and hobby use; revenue and business decisions require Business/Enterprise. Raw dataset redistribution and API substitutes are prohibited. This gives a plausible permission path for a genuinely free collector tool, not every open-source monetization model.

The [licensing explanation](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/licensing) permits caching inside one's product, makes attribution optional, grants no image rights, limits individual sold-listing detail to Business, and expressly makes no representation about upstream sourcing or rights. It refuses sourcing/authorization assurances. Thus it cannot substantiate marketplace authorization here; asking support for an assurance it explicitly refuses is not a concrete resolution plan. Per-user native-client use, account-key ownership, currency/shipping semantics and acceptable upstream evidence still need resolution. **Verdict: investigate only as labeled aggregates under an explicit evidence/risk decision; not verified free comps.**

## Recommendations for the human price-evidence decision

**Proposal:** keep recognition, exact-printing matching and price availability as three separate outcomes. Recognizing a PSA label must not produce a raw-price substitution, a generic “grade 9” must not become “PSA 9,” and a null price must not become zero. Catalog identity should retain language, set, number, edition/finish and provider IDs; slab matching additionally needs grader, grade and qualifiers/designations. Whether a catalog supports all desired printings remains unmeasured.

**Proposal:** distinguish the following fallback classes explicitly. This table compares product options; it does not select one.

| Evidence class | What it can honestly provide | What remains missing / scope effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sourced valuation / aggregate | A named provider's guide or sales-derived estimate, dated, with exact supported matching and limitations. Conditional free graded aggregates exist in the candidates above. | Individual auditable sales, demonstrated upstream rights, full coverage and condition/printing confidence are not established. Human acceptance is required. |
| Labeled asking prices / current bids | Active offers for matched raw cards or slabs, with item links, currency and shipping where available. | Not evidence a buyer paid that amount. eBay access/terms/native-client gates still apply; this alone cannot substantiate fair market value. |
| User-opened research links | Open the source's card/search page for the collector to inspect raw or slab evidence, including eBay/PriceCharting/Pokedata where accessible. | No automatic sourced number, no imported sales and no five-second decision guarantee. Access/sign-in can interrupt. |
| Recognition-only when evidence is unavailable | Identify the card/slab and state pricing unavailable, preserving uncertainty. | No fair-price guidance for that case. Making this the normal slab experience changes the launch promise unless explicitly accepted. |

**Proposal:** a price result should preserve evidence kind, provider and upstream, product/source URL, exact printing and language match, raw condition or grader/grade, original currency, source observation time, fetch time, aggregation window and sample count when known. Store shipping/tax/fees as included/excluded/unknown; do not assume zero or compare converted markets as equivalent. Cardmarket lacks these matching dimensions; wrappers do not manufacture them by returning an English card name. Suggested expiry thresholds and minimum sample counts remain human policy, not facts measured here.

**Unmeasured hypotheses:** local catalog caching may reduce lookup delay; per-user keys may suffice for light use; free aggregates may cover common PSA slabs. None was tested for the five-second workflow, release-wide coverage or sustained auction use. A future authorized validation should sample vintage first-edition/shadowless/unlimited, modern normal/reverse/holo, promo/stamped printings, raw conditions and the selected grader/grade matrix, verifying source links, dates, currency, null behavior and API entitlement. Do not create a signup task until a candidate's purpose and permission gate are selected.

## Unresolved decisions and permission questions

1. **Evidence standard:** must fair-price guidance include individual dated sold comps, or are named aggregates acceptable? No free, permission-established per-sale path was confirmed for both raw and slabs.
2. **Graded launch promise:** which graders, grade scales, qualifiers and printing variants are required, and what happens when no valid evidence exists? Their selection must not silently remove slabs from launch.
3. **Source authority:** is a provider's own aggregate methodology enough when upstream rights are explicitly undisclosed, or is marketplace authorization required? PokemonPriceTracker's published refusal to provide assurance constrains this decision.
4. **Distribution/revenue:** can the app remain entirely non-revenue, including no ads/affiliate links/sponsorship or product-linked donations, where a chosen source requires that? Open-source code alone does not confer free data rights. JustTCG's shipping guidance is an additional obstacle even for free distribution.
5. **Catalog/artwork:** approve a catalog only after testing exact printings. Decide whether images are needed and establish display/cache/bundling rights independently of software/data licenses.
6. **Operational contract:** choose market/currency, condition basis, shipping/fees treatment, expiry policy, minimum evidence and per-user quota behavior. Public rate limits are ceilings, not latency guarantees. Native per-user credentials and cache permissions must be documented for the selected source rather than inferred from browser examples.
