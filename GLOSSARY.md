# Auction companion

An English Pokémon card auction companion for collectors checking whether a bid is fair.

## Language

**Card printing**:
A particular release of a card, distinguished by its set, collector number, language, edition, and finish or other variant. Cards sharing a name can be different printings.
_Avoid_: Card name as a complete identity

**Raw card**:
A card being valued without an assigned third-party slab grade. Its physical condition is separate from its printing.

**Graded slab**:
A card in a grading company's labeled holder, with its stated grade. Reading the label does not establish authenticity or verify that the card deserves that grade.

**Comparable sale**:
A completed transaction sufficiently matched to the card printing and relevant condition or slab grade to inform its value. An active offer to sell is not a comparable sale.
_Avoid_: Comp for an asking price or an aggregate valuation

**Asking price**:
The amount a seller requests for an item, without evidence that a buyer paid it.

**Catalog valuation**:
A source's summarized value for a card and condition or grade. It is distinct from an individual completed transaction.

**Price evidence**:
Attributable source information used to assess a card's value, such as comparable sales, catalog valuations, or clearly identified asking prices. These evidence types are not interchangeable.

**Auction lot**:
The item or group of items offered in one auction. MVP valuation coverage is limited to single-card lots.
