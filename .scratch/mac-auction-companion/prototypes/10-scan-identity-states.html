When the card is uncertain — or already gone

A clickable state model for the auction companion. It explores one question: how should the app avoid confidently pricing the wrong printing, grade, or lot when the video is ambiguous or you scan again immediately?

Drive the free-play buttons, or run a guided walkthrough below. Every card, label, AI response, and price is synthetic.

Prototype only
No screen capture, AI request, price lookup, or saved data. The price numbers are invented examples, not market evidence. This file answers a design question; it is not production code.
One live scanA new scan supersedes the old one. Late answers are rejected by scan ID.
Ambiguity withholds priceShow alternatives and visible evidence. A tap confirms a printing; the model never picks for you.
No grade guessingIf a required label field is unreadable, ask for a closer scan instead of inventing a grade.
No confidence theatreCalibrated confidence is unavailable, so the model's self-reported percentage is not shown as correctness.
Last scan, not liveThe app cannot detect lot changes. Every retained result stays labeled with its scan.
No silent retryFailures wait for a manual, billable retry. Reuse of a prior identity is explicit and limited to the preceding scan.
