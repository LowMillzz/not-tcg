## Question

Before selecting the capture architecture, run a disposable Mac validation on the chosen [launch environments](14-launch-mac-and-viewing-environments.md) to settle the specific facts left unproved by documentation. This task unblocks an architecture decision; it is not production app implementation.

Use the fleet computer-use playbook and linked macmini environment, verify host/account, and keep the parent project on devbox. Check whether that Mac can exercise the chosen OS matrix; record untestable targets rather than extrapolating. Use synthetic or appropriately authorized public content, keep any account interaction manual, and create no platform account, signing identity, or inference bill. Access/content prerequisites must be supplied before dependent experiments; do not interpret elapsed time as consent.

Checklist for the driving session:

1. Test repeated stills after picker selection with broad Screen Recording permission absent; end/reselect, close/reopen, relaunch, deny and revoke. Record whether consent persists and when capture stops. If the chosen path needs a persistent stream or broader permission, return that change to the human.
2. On the agreed viewing platform/browser/region, verify current video pixels and manual bid controls, including readable raw details and slab labels. Capture availability and buyer-flow availability must both hold; record unsupported combinations without a workaround that changes scope.
3. Test crop coordinates and freshness when moving/resizing, changing scale/Spaces and covering/minimizing/closing the source. Confirm that overlays are excluded, no full-screen fallback occurs, and capture/result presentation does not steal manual-bidding focus.
4. Measure trigger-to-crop timing for initial and repeat captures with a changing time target. Record actual environment and limitations. This is not a recognition or pricing benchmark and cannot establish end-to-end five-second performance.

Write a concise evidence artifact and handoff with goal, host, repository revision, observations, verification and next action; do not retain private screenshots, authentication material or full chat logs. Resolve only when the facts needed to choose capture/permission strategy are established or a documented incompatibility has been returned for the user's decision. Packaged-production release testing belongs to later release acceptance, outside this task.
