## Question

Within the user-confirmed requirement for both raw and graded English Pokémon singles, which grader companies, grade labels, printing attributes, and catalog boundaries are supported at launch?

Agree the identity needed before showing a price: card name, set, collector number, language, edition, finish/variant, raw versus slab, and for slabs the grader and visible grade. Decide treatment of qualifiers, subgrades, altered/authentic-only slabs, reprints, promos, illegible labels, and a seller showing multiple cards. Separate reading a printed grade from verifying slab authenticity or assigning a grade to raw footage.

Clarify whether raw condition is explicitly unknown unless supplied, what happens when a printing is unresolvable, and whether launch coverage means recognition plus useful price evidence for each supported type. Use the free-data research to expose coverage gaps; do not silently redefine 'both raw and graded'. Capture settled domain terms in the glossary.

### First interview round — answered

The user explicitly chose:

- PSA slabs plus raw cards first; CGC/BGS follow later.
- Raw condition remains unknown unless the collector supplies it. Any source price's condition basis must be shown; do not infer Near Mint from the stream.
- Resolve the exact printing and relevant slab grade before displaying a matched price. Offer identity candidates or a rescan when the match is unresolved; do not substitute a lookalike printing or grade.

These are accepted boundaries, not source-selection or performance decisions. Aggregate-versus-sold evidence, price-condition presentation, and missing-price behavior remain in the separate price-evidence ticket.
